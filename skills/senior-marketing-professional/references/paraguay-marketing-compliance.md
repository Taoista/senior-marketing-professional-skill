# Paraguay Marketing Compliance

Read this reference for campaigns, ecommerce, promotions, CRM, direct marketing, personal-data use, regulated products, comparative claims, or content rights in Paraguay.

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. It was reviewed against official sources on November 5, 2026. Laws, regulations, authorities, and official interpretations can change; verify current primary sources and obtain qualified Paraguayan legal review for material decisions.

## Consumer information, offers, and advertising

Use [Ley N° 1334/1998 de Defensa del Consumidor y del Usuario](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/897/ley) as the primary consumer-advertising checkpoint.

- Articles 8 to 10 require truthful, sufficient, clear, precise, and visible information about the offer. Prices must include taxes and be stated precisely in legal tender.
- An offer binds the provider during its stated validity. If no validity period is shown, do not assume artificial urgency or scarcity.
- Articles 35 to 39 prohibit misleading and abusive advertising, restrict certain comparative advertising, and place responsibility on the advertiser to prove the truth of advertising claims.
- Material conditions must be visible where the claim or promotion is presented. Do not hide minimum purchase amounts, exclusions, stock limits, speed reductions, renewal terms, or other restrictions in an inaccessible disclaimer.
- Advertising for tobacco, alcoholic beverages, medicines, and stimulant beverages is also subject to sector-specific laws.

Also apply [Ley N° 6366](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9529/texto), which modifies Ley N° 1334 for financed offers and credit advertising:

- show the Costo Total de Crédito (CTC) when an offer is financed in installments;
- whenever advertising states an installment amount or reference interest rate, show the CTC with comparable prominence, including typography, size, placement, duration, diction, repetition, and audio level as applicable;
- retain the calculation source and approval for the advertised CTC;
- escalate financial and credit advertising to the responsible legal, compliance, or regulated-business owner before launch.

SEDECO's [consumer-rights guidance](https://sedeco.gov.py/derechos-del-consumidor/) reinforces clear information and protection from misleading advertising. Its enforcement examples show that hidden minimum-purchase conditions and claims such as "unlimited" without visible restrictions can create enforcement risk.

## Ecommerce and electronic communications

Use [Ley N° 4868/2013 de Comercio Electrónico](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/961/ley-n-4868-comercio-) and Decreto N° 1165/2014. The [official MIC ecommerce framework](https://www.mic.gov.py/reclamos-sobre-comercio-electronico-y-servicios-de-confianza/) also lists the current authority, regulations, and complaint channel.

Before checkout or contracting, verify that the experience clearly presents:

- provider identity, address, email, and telephone;
- privacy and security information;
- product or service characteristics;
- delivery method, timing, responsibility, and cost;
- guarantee, cancellation, return, exchange, and refund conditions;
- price, currency, payment method, final value, freight, and other costs;
- a way to identify and correct input errors;
- an express confirmation step so silence is not treated as consent;
- the applicable consumer-protection framework and authority contact.

For electronic commercial messages:

- identify the message as commercial and identify the person or organization on whose behalf it is sent;
- offer a simple and free way to object at collection and in every commercial communication;
- label unsolicited commercial communications as such when the legal conditions for sending them are met;
- use recipient data obtained without violating privacy rights;
- retain evidence of opt-outs and suppress future sends.

The law also establishes a withdrawal regime for certain electronic transactions and specific exceptions. Do not state a universal return promise or denial without checking the transaction and current rule.

## Mobile calls and messages

Use [Ley N° 5830/2017](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9734/ley-n-5830-proh-be-la-publicidad-no-autorizada-por-los-usuarios-titulares-de-telefon-a-m-vil), Decreto N° 8000/2017, and the official [SEDECO No Molestar portal](https://nomolestar.sedeco.gov.py/) for calls, SMS, multimedia messages, WhatsApp, and comparable mobile contact.

- Check the national register administered by SEDECO before a mobile commercial contact when applicable.
- Do not contact a registered number unless a legal exception applies.
- Account for the operational waiting period stated by SEDECO after a number is registered, while treating a direct opt-out communicated to the company as immediately actionable.
- A current contractual relationship is not blanket permission: the contact must relate strictly to that relationship and comply with applicable conditions.
- Express authorization must be demonstrable. A brokered, scraped, or purchased list is not proof by itself.
- Keep the register check, consent evidence, campaign source, sender, date, and suppression outcome auditable.

## Personal data and the 2027 transition

[Ley N° 6534/2020 de Protección de Datos Personales Crediticios](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9417/ley-n-6534-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-credit) is part of the current framework and repealed Ley N° 1682/2001 and its amendments. It includes requirements concerning consent, purpose, data quality, security, confidentiality, rights, and credit information. Treat financial and credit campaigns as heightened-risk work.

[Ley N° 7593/2025 de Protección de Datos Personales](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/12924/ley-n-7593-proteccion-de-datos-personales) creates a comprehensive regime. Article 57 provides that it enters into force 24 months after official publication; [MITIC states that it will take effect in late 2027](https://mitic.gov.py/mitic-informa-sobre-el-alcance-de-la-ley-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales/). Do not describe it as fully effective before then, but prepare operations for its requirements.

For marketing data, document:

- controller and processors;
- data categories and source;
- purpose and applicable legal basis or authorization;
- notice delivered to the person;
- audience creation, profiling, and automated decisions;
- recipients and international transfers;
- retention and deletion rules;
- security and incident handling;
- procedures for access, rectification, suppression, opposition, and portability;
- special protection for children and sensitive data.

Do not upload customer lists to advertising platforms or create matched/lookalike audiences until the source, authorization, notices, platform terms, and deletion process have been checked.

## Intellectual property and competition

Use [Ley N° 1294/1998 de Marcas](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/862/de-marcas) and [Ley N° 1328/1998 de Derecho de Autor y Derechos Conexos](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/908/ley-n-1328-derecho-de-autor-y-derechos) for brand names, logos, slogans, photographs, music, video, copy, software, and other creative assets.

Keep evidence of ownership, license scope, territory, media, duration, modification rights, talent or likeness releases, and third-party approvals. A public or easily downloadable asset is not automatically licensed for commercial use.

Consult [CONACOM's official regulatory index](https://conacom.gov.py/normativa/) and Ley N° 4956/2013 when work involves competitor coordination, pricing practices, exclusivity, market-sensitive information, dominant-position concerns, or other competition issues.

## Campaign approval record

For material campaigns, record:

1. Audience, channel, geography, and regulated category.
2. Claims and supporting evidence.
3. Full price, taxes, costs, validity, inventory, and restrictions.
4. Promotion or contest terms and approval owner.
5. Data source, authorization, notice, suppression, and retention status.
6. Mobile do-not-contact check when applicable.
7. Rights and licenses for every material asset and likeness.
8. Legal or specialist reviewer, decision, conditions, and review date.

Recheck official sources before launch when the campaign is high-spend, high-reach, regulated, uses sensitive data, targets minors, makes health or financial claims, or could materially affect customers.
